芯片 · 头条Chips · Top story

黄仁勋辞任英伟达 CEO，CFO 克雷斯出任临时 CEOJensen Huang resigns as Nvidia CEO; CFO Colette Kress named interim CEO

11 月 12 日，英伟达宣布黄仁勋即刻辞去总裁、首席执行官及董事职务，首席财务官 Colette Kress 出任临时 CEO，公司已启动正式继任者遴选。此事发生在 Megaspeed 芯片转运调查持续发酵之际，黄仁勋还被要求在感恩节前赴美国国会作证。此前两个交易日，英伟达股价累计下跌逾 9%，市值蒸发超过 4200 亿美元，云厂商已开始评估谷歌、AMD 的替代芯片方案。On November 12, Nvidia announced that Jensen Huang had resigned as president, CEO and board member with immediate effect, with CFO Colette Kress named interim CEO while a permanent successor is sought. The move comes as the Megaspeed chip-diversion investigation widens; Huang has also been summoned to testify before the U.S. Congress before Thanksgiving. Nvidia shares had fallen more than 9% over the prior two sessions, wiping out over $420 billion in market value, and cloud providers have begun evaluating Google and AMD alternatives.

美国国会两党推动收紧对华 AI 芯片出口U.S. Congress pushes tighter AI chip export curbs on China

众议院议长约翰逊表示，国会在限制对华芯片出口上正形成共识。议员们推动至少四项法案：AI Overwatch Act（国会掌握出口审批权）、Chip Security Act（芯片定位追踪防走私）、Match Act（施压盟友限制设备出口）与 Remote Access Security Act（限制通过海外数据中心远程租用英伟达芯片）。House Speaker Mike Johnson said Congress is reaching consensus on limiting chip exports to China. Lawmakers are advancing at least four bills: the AI Overwatch Act (congressional say over export approvals), the Chip Security Act (chip location tracking), the Match Act (pressuring allies on equipment sales) and the Remote Access Security Act (restricting rental of Nvidia chips via overseas data centers).

芯片Chips

中芯国际：AI 芯片供应链紧张将持续到 2027 年SMIC: AI chip supply chain stays tight through 2027

中芯国际联席 CEO 赵海军在财报沟通中表示，AI 行业仍处早期、需求强劲，受数据中心与私有云需求拉动，AI 相关半导体供应链紧张将持续到 2027 年，存储芯片供需预计到 2028 年中下旬才趋于平衡。公司第三季度产能利用率升至 97.3%，接近满产。SMIC co-CEO Zhao Haijun told an earnings call that AI demand remains strong and the industry is still in its early stages: the AI semiconductor supply chain will stay tight through 2027 on data-center and private-cloud demand, with memory supply and demand balancing only toward mid-to-late 2028. The foundry's Q3 utilization rose to 97.3%, near full capacity.

The open questions are software ecosystems and advanced-node supply.

SpaceX 拟投超 740 亿美元建两座 AI 超算园区SpaceX plans $74B+ for two AI supercomputing campuses

SpaceX 11 月 12 日宣布，计划在得克萨斯州与亚利桑那州投资超过 740 亿美元，建设两座面向人工智能的超级计算数据中心园区。得州项目选址 Starbase 附近约 444 英亩自有土地并自建燃气发电；亚利桑那项目约 500 亿美元，仍待县政府批准，已遭社区反对，听证会定于 12 月举行。SpaceX announced on November 12 that it plans to invest more than $74 billion in two AI supercomputing data-center campuses in Texas and Arizona. The Texas site covers roughly 444 acres near Starbase with its own gas-fired power; the roughly $50 billion Arizona project still needs county approval, faces community opposition, and goes to a hearing in December.

Anthropic IPO 询价首日足额认购，11 月 19 日挂牌Anthropic IPO fully subscribed on day one; trading starts Nov 19

正在冲刺上市的 Anthropic 询价首日即获足额认购：发行价区间 70–78 美元，拟募资约 610 亿美元，对应估值约 1.1 万亿至 1.3 万亿美元。预计 11 月 18 日定价、11 月 19 日以代码「ANT」在纳斯达克开始交易，有望成为第一家以 AI 为主业的上市公司。本周它刚发布了 Claude Sheets 等办公套件。Anthropic's IPO book-building was fully subscribed on its first day: a $70–78 price range, roughly $61 billion to be raised, and a valuation of about $1.1–1.3 trillion. Nebius 上调 2027 年支出计划至 400 亿美元Nebius lifts 2027 spending plan to $40B

云厂商 Nebius 在财报会上称，2027 年资本支出计划将达 400 亿美元，远超华尔街预期，资金靠客户预付款与合同担保贷款解决：第三季度签约 120 亿美元，对应资本开支已 100% 由预付款覆盖。同时因新泽西 Vineland 等数据中心延期，公司将 2026 年收入预期下调 2 亿美元至约 30 亿美元。Cloud provider Nebius told its earnings call that 2027 capital spending will reach $40 billion — far above Wall Street expectations — funded by customer prepayments and contract-backed loans: Q3 signings totaled $12 billion, with prepayments covering 100% of the related capex. Delays at sites including Vineland, New Jersey, led it to cut 2026 revenue guidance by $200 million to about $3 billion.
