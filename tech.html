Anthropic IPO 认购约六倍超额，有望成为史上最大 IPOAnthropic IPO about six times oversubscribed in what could be the biggest listing ever

据彭博社报道，Anthropic 首次公开募股已获得约六倍于可供认购股份的订单：公司拟募资最高约 644 亿美元，另有 260 亿美元私募配售，目标完全摊薄估值约 1.4 万亿美元。英伟达已同意在私募配售中认购约 100 亿美元股份。公司截至 10 月 31 日的收入年化 run rate 达 810 亿美元。预计 11 月 18 日定价、11 月 19 日以代码「ANT」在纳斯达克挂牌。Bloomberg reported that Anthropic's IPO has drawn about six times the orders for the shares on offer: the company is seeking up to $64.4 billion in the offering plus a $26 billion private placement, targeting a fully diluted valuation of about $1.4 trillion. Nvidia has agreed to buy about $10 billion of shares in the placement. Anthropic's revenue run rate reached $81 billion as of October 31. Pricing is expected on November 18, with trading on Nasdaq under "ANT" from November 19.

谷歌发布 Gemini 4 Hydrogen，主打快速执行与多模型协作Google launches Gemini 4 Hydrogen for fast execution and multi-model work

谷歌 11 月 12 日发布 Gemini 4 Hydrogen，定位为面向日常编码与智能体工作流的快速执行模型，全 100 万 token 上下文统一定价为每百万输入 token 1.50 美元、输出 7.50 美元。官方称其在 Artificial Analysis 智能指数默认档得分 51 分，超过旗舰 Argon 的 50 分；Terminal-Bench 4.1 得分 57%，约为前代 3.8 Flash 的三倍。配套的 Antigravity Orchestrator 可在同一会话内动态切换 Carbon 与 Hydrogen 模型。Google launched Gemini 4 Hydrogen on November 12 as a fast execution model for everyday coding and agentic workflows, priced flat at $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens across its full 1-million-token context. Google says it scores 51 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at its default setting, ahead of flagship Argon at 50, and 57% on Terminal-Bench 4.1 — roughly triple the 18.2% of its predecessor, 3.8 Flash. The companion Antigravity Orchestrator switches between Carbon and Hydrogen models within a single session.

美国国会加速收紧对华 AI 芯片出口，四项法案拟并入国防授权法U.S. Congress accelerates tighter AI chip export curbs on China; four bills eyed for defense act

据路透社报道，美国众议院议长约翰逊 11 月 12 日表示，国会在限制对华芯片出口上正形成共识；两党议员推动至少四项法案，并有意将其并入必须通过的 2027 财年国防授权法案。四项法案分别涉及国会对出口审批的监督权、芯片定位追踪、施压盟友限制设备出口，以及封堵经海外数据中心远程租用芯片的漏洞。据报道，OpenAI 已私下表态支持相关限制措施。Reuters reported that House Speaker Mike Johnson said on November 12 that Congress is building consensus on limiting chip exports to China, with lawmakers from both parties advancing at least four bills and aiming to attach them to the must-pass fiscal 2027 defense authorization act. The bills cover congressional oversight of export approvals, chip location tracking, pressure on allies over equipment sales, and closing the loophole of renting chips via overseas data centers. OpenAI has privately backed the measures, according to a policy briefing.

云计算Cloud

微软收缩外购算力：不再续签甲骨文云合同，转向自建数据中心Microsoft pulls back from rented AI capacity, letting Oracle cloud deal lapse

据彭博社报道，微软决定不再续签与甲骨文的云合同，并正重新评估与 CoreWeave、Nebius 等新云厂商的合作，优先自建数据中心。微软是新云厂商的大客户——今年第二季度占 CoreWeave 收入的 36%，与 Nebius 签有总额 174 亿美元、至 2031 年的合同。微软自研 AI 芯片 Maia 300 预计 2027 年量产，届时更多工作负载将转向自有芯片。Bloomberg reported that Microsoft has chosen not to renew its cloud deal with Oracle and is re-evaluating contracts with neocloud providers including CoreWeave and Nebius, prioritizing its own data centers. Microsoft is a major customer for the group — it accounted for 36% of CoreWeave's revenue in the second quarter and holds a $17.4 billion contract with Nebius running through 2031. Its custom AI chip, Maia 300, is expected to ramp in 2027, shifting more workloads onto its own silicon.

Cerebras 签下微软为第三家超大规模客户，但 OpenAI 首批算力交付已落后Cerebras signs Microsoft as third hyperscaler, but lags on first OpenAI capacity tranche

AI 芯片公司 Cerebras 宣布签下微软作为第三家超大规模客户，在手合同算力达 700 MW、未交付订单 261 亿美元；但新产能 12 月才开始爬坡，本季度云收入预计持平。另据 Crypto Briefing 报道，Cerebras 在与 OpenAI 的 750 MW 推理算力协议上已落后：首批 250 MW 须在 2026 年底前交付，公司称数据中心签约进度慢于预期。AI chipmaker Cerebras has signed Microsoft as its third hyperscaler customer, with 700 MW under contract and a $26.1 billion backlog — though new capacity only starts ramping in December, leaving cloud revenue flat this quarter. Crypto Briefing reported separately that Cerebras is behind on its 750 MW inference agreement with OpenAI: the first 250 MW tranche is due by the end of 2026, and the company blames slower-than-expected data-center contracting.

中国 AI 服务器商 xFusion 通过深交所审核，拟募资 12 亿美元上市Chinese AI server maker xFusion clears review for $1.2B Shenzhen IPO

据路透社报道，中国 AI 服务器制造商 xFusion 已通过深交所上市委审核，拟在创业板上市募资 80 亿元（约 12 亿美元），中信证券担任保荐机构。xFusion 生产包括 AI 服务器在内的算力基础设施。上市委 11 月 12 日的文件确认公司符合发行、上市与信息披露要求。Reuters reported that Chinese AI server maker xFusion Digital Technologies has cleared a Shenzhen Stock Exchange listing review for a planned ChiNext IPO raising 8 billion yuan (about $1.2 billion), with CITIC Securities as sponsor. xFusion makes computing infrastructure including AI servers. A November 12 filing from the exchange's listing committee confirmed the company met issuance, listing and disclosure requirements.
