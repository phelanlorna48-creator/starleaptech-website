Anthropic IPO 定价 83 美元，今日纳斯达克挂牌、估值约 1.5 万亿美元Anthropic prices IPO at $83, debuts on Nasdaq today at a roughly $1.5 trillion valuation

Anthropic 11 月 18 日确认 IPO 定价每股 83 美元，高于 70 至 78 美元的发行价区间，本次发售 9.8 亿股 A 类股、募资规模达 813.4 亿美元，较原计划的 8.25 亿股有所扩大。据彭博社报道，认购需求一度达到发行量的约六倍；按此定价，公司估值约 1.5 万亿美元。股票将于 11 月 19 日在纳斯达克以代码 ANT 开始交易，发行预计 11 月 20 日完成交割。Anthropic confirmed on November 18 that it has priced its IPO at $83 per share, above the $70–$78 range, raising $81.34 billion on the sale of 980 million Class A shares, enlarged from the 825 million originally planned. Bloomberg reported demand reached about six times the shares on offer, and the pricing implies a valuation of roughly $1.5 trillion. Shares begin trading on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT on November 19, with the offering expected to close on November 20.

Google 发布 Gemini 4 Neon，瞄准编码与知识工作Google launches Gemini 4 Neon for coding and knowledge work

Google DeepMind 11 月 18 日发布 Gemini 4 家族新成员 Gemini 4 Neon，定位介于面向高难度长周期任务的 Carbon 与面向日常快任务的 Hydrogen 之间，主打复杂软件工程、知识工作与科学应用。API 定价为每百万输入 token 3 美元、输出 15 美元，上下文窗口 100 万 token；据 Google 称，Neon 在 Artificial Analysis 智能指数中得分 61、排名第三。模型即日起向 Gemini API 付费用户及 Google AI Pro 与 Ultra 订阅用户开放。Google DeepMind released Gemini 4 Neon on November 18, the newest member of the Gemini 4 family, positioned between Carbon, built for the hardest long-horizon projects, and Hydrogen, built for fast everyday tasks. It targets complex software engineering, knowledge work and science, priced at $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens, with a 1-million-token context window. Google says Neon scores 61 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, ranking third overall. It is rolling out now to paid Gemini API users and to Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers.

芯片Chips

OpenAI 模型改写电路设计，AMD 旗舰 GPU 效率最高提升 14%OpenAI models rewrite circuit designs, lifting AMD flagship GPU efficiency by up to 14%

OpenAI 11 月 18 日发布技术报告称，其模型通过重写 SystemVerilog 电路代码，在 AMD 旗舰 GPU 知识产权模块上实现了最高 14% 的功耗、性能与面积（PPA）综合提升，相当于把约一年的优化工作压缩到不到五个月完成；AMD 已将部分改动纳入送交制造的设计。在自研推理芯片 Jalapeño 上，模型提出的改动使向量单元与矩阵单元的标准单元面积分别减少约 13% 与 10%。OpenAI 还宣布与 Synopsys 合作训练专用于芯片设计工具的 GPT-Synopsys 模型。OpenAI said on November 18 that its models rewrote SystemVerilog circuit code to deliver up to 14% gains in power, performance and area (PPA) on AMD's flagship GPU intellectual property — roughly a year of design optimization compressed into under five months — and AMD has included some of the changes in a design sent for manufacturing. On Jalapeño, OpenAI's own inference chip, model-proposed changes cut standard-cell area by about 13% in the vector unit and 10% in the matrix unit. OpenAI also announced a partnership with Synopsys to train GPT-Synopsys, a model built to drive chip-design tools.

Palo Alto Networks 45 亿美元全股票收购 Applied ComputePalo Alto Networks to buy Applied Compute in a $4.5 billion all-stock deal

Palo Alto Networks 11 月 18 日宣布达成最终协议，以全股票方式收购开源权重模型训练与推理平台 Applied Compute，交易价值 45 亿美元，预计 2027 财年第三季度完成交割，尚待监管审批。Applied Compute 联合创始人兼 CEO Yash Patil 将在交割后出任 Palo Alto Networks 联席首席 AI 官。同日公司还公布 2027 财年第一财季营收 34.4 亿美元，同比增长 39%。Palo Alto Networks announced on November 18 a definitive agreement to acquire Applied Compute, a platform for post-training and inference of open-weight AI models, in an all-stock deal valued at $4.5 billion, expected to close in its fiscal third quarter of 2027 pending regulatory clearance. Applied Compute co-founder and CEO Yash Patil will become Palo Alto Networks' Co-Chief AI Officer after the close. The company also reported fiscal first-quarter 2027 revenue of $3.44 billion, up 39% year on year.

微软与英特尔扩大 Azure 合作，自研芯片采用 EMIB 封装Microsoft and Intel expand Azure pact, with Microsoft custom chips adopting EMIB packaging

微软与英特尔 11 月 18 日宣布扩大合作，签署长期协议共同推进 Azure 云与 AI 基础设施；微软成为首家宣布采用英特尔 EMIB 先进封装技术的公司，将其用于自研芯片，包括下一代 Maia AI 加速器与 Cobalt CPU。英特尔同时承诺向微软长期供应至强处理器，包括代号 Diamond Rapids 的下一代至强 7 服务器 CPU。双方还将在 CXL 内存扩展、QuickAssist 加速与机密计算方面深入合作。Microsoft and Intel announced an expanded partnership on November 18, signing a long-term agreement to advance Azure cloud and AI infrastructure. Microsoft became the first company to announce adoption of Intel's EMIB advanced packaging technology for its custom chips, including the next-generation Maia AI accelerator and Cobalt CPU. Intel also committed to long-term Xeon supply for Microsoft, including the next-generation Xeon 7 server CPU, codenamed Diamond Rapids. The two companies will also collaborate on CXL memory expansion, QuickAssist acceleration and confidential computing.

马来西亚要求数据中心遵守美国 AI 芯片新规Malaysia orders data centers to follow new U.S. AI chip rules

马来西亚投资、贸易及工业部长 Johari Abdul Ghani 11 月 18 日表示，该国数据中心须遵守美国商务部新出台的 AI 芯片出口限制；政府将通知全国 117 个已获批数据中心项目，其中 55 个已投入运营。新规将于 11 月 19 日生效，要求向马来西亚等国出口先进芯片须持许可证，并对向中国企业提供大规模算力的数据中心出口设限。Malaysia's Investment, Trade and Industry Minister Johari Abdul Ghani said on November 18 that the country's data centers must comply with the U.S. Commerce Department's new AI chip export restrictions, and the government will notify all 117 approved data center projects in the country, 55 of which are already operational. The rule takes effect on November 19, requiring licenses to export advanced chips to Malaysia and other countries, and restricting exports to data centers supplying large-scale compute to Chinese firms.
