比特币跌破 6 万美元，创 2024 年 9 月以来新低，24 小时约 20 亿美元杠杆多头被强平Bitcoin falls below $60,000 to its lowest since September 2024 as about $2 billion in leveraged longs are liquidated in 24 hours

比特币 11 月 20 日跌破 6 万美元关口，为 7 月以来首次，盘中最低触及 57,716 美元，创 2024 年 9 月以来最低水平。据《商业时报》报道，比特币当天的暴跌在 24 小时内触发了约 20 亿美元的杠杆多头仓位强平。抛售在周末延续：据 CryptoPotato 报道，比特币 11 月 21 日上午一度跌至约 55,500 美元。分析认为，此轮下跌主要受美股 AI 抛售带动的风险资产全面回撤驱动，而非加密市场自身的问题。Bitcoin fell below $60,000 on November 20 for the first time since July, touching an intraday low of $57,716 — its lowest since September 2024. The Business Times reported that the slide triggered the liquidation of roughly $2 billion in leveraged long crypto positions over 24 hours. The sell-off continued into the weekend: CryptoPotato reported Bitcoin falling to about $55,500 on Saturday morning. Analysts attributed the drop mainly to a broad retreat from risk assets driven by the sell-off in U.S. AI stocks, rather than to problems specific to crypto.

AI · 模型AI · Models

Thinking Machines 开源 Inkling 2 Mini：264 亿参数循环模型，可在 32GB 笔记本本地运行Thinking Machines open-sources Inkling 2 Mini, a 26.4B-parameter looped model that runs on a 32GB laptop

Thinking Machines Lab 11 月 20 日发布开源权重模型 Inkling 2 Mini：总参数 264 亿、每 token 激活 34 亿，采用循环 Transformer 架构，中间 12 层共享权重重复运行三次，约 20GB 的检查点可在 32GB 内存的 MacBook Pro 或单块桌面 GPU 上离线运行。据 VentureBeat 报道，该模型在 SWE-bench Verified 上得分 79.2%，以 Apache 2.0 许可证发布，上下文窗口达 100 万 token。Thinking Machines Lab released Inkling 2 Mini on November 20, an open-weights model with 26.4 billion total parameters and 3.4 billion active per token. Its looped-transformer design runs the middle twelve layers three times with shared weights, and the roughly 20GB checkpoint can run offline on a 32GB MacBook Pro or a single desktop GPU. VentureBeat reported the model scores 79.2% on SWE-bench Verified; it is released under an Apache 2.0 licence with a context window of one million tokens.

数据中心 · 成本Data Centers · Costs

数据中心算力交付成本同比上涨 40%，电网拥堵与电价推高 AI 算力价格Data centre compute costs jump 40% year on year as grid congestion and power prices push up AI compute

据彭博社报道，数据中心算力交付成本同比上涨 40%，主因是电力供应紧张与电网容量约束。在美国最大的数据中心聚集区 PJM 电网，2026 年上半年输电拥堵成本同比激增 43% 至 60 亿美元；弗吉尼亚州 2019 至 2025 年间商业售电量增加近 3000 万兆瓦时，能源信息署称主要由数据中心推动。亚太地区也正收紧新项目审批与并网规则。The cost of delivering data-centre compute has risen 40% year on year, Bloomberg reported, driven by power availability and capacity constraints. In PJM, the U.S. grid region hosting the largest concentration of data centres, transmission congestion costs jumped 43% to $6 billion in the first half of 2026; commercial electricity sales in Virginia rose by nearly 30 million megawatt-hours between 2019 and 2025, which the Energy Information Administration attributed largely to data centres. Governments across Asia-Pacific are also tightening approvals and connection rules for new projects.

AI · 开源AI · Open Source

腾讯开源 7700 亿参数多模态大模型 Hy4，上下文达 100 万 tokenTencent open-sources Hy4, a 770B-parameter multimodal model with a 1M-token context

据 AI Compass 报道，腾讯 11 月 20 日在 Hugging Face、ModelScope 与 GitCode 上开源了 7700 亿参数多模态混合专家模型 Hy4 及其 FP8 量化权重，同时发布预训练版 Hy4-base 与 33 亿参数的 DFly2 草稿模型，上下文窗口达 100 万 token，采用腾讯 Hy 许可证。这是本周第二款重磅开源模型，与 Thinking Machines 的 Inkling 2 Mini 同日发布。Tencent open-sourced its 770-billion-parameter multimodal mixture-of-experts model Hy4 and its FP8 weights on November 20, AI Compass reported, publishing them on Hugging Face, ModelScope and GitCode alongside a pre-trained Hy4-base and a 3.3-billion-parameter DFly2 drafter model, with a context window of one million tokens under the Tencent Hy License. It was the second major open release of the week, landing the same day as Thinking Machines' Inkling 2 Mini.
