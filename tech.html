英伟达董事会戏剧性反转：黄仁勋辞职 11 天后恢复 CEO 职务，股价大涨逾 30%Nvidia board reverses course: Jensen Huang reinstated as CEO 11 days after resigning, shares surge more than 30%

英伟达 11 月 23 日宣布，董事会一致投票决定，立即恢复黄仁勋的总裁、首席执行官及董事职务。黄仁勋 11 月 12 日因承认婚外情辞职，其中一位对象被指涉嫌为中国从事间谍活动；董事会表示，在与美国政府高层沟通后，确认其复职不存在国家安全风险。针对黄仁勋是否成为境外情报行动目标的独立调查仍在继续，公司还将新设国家安全与出口管制委员会并招聘首席合规官；原临时 CEO、首席财务官 Colette Kress 回归原职。消息公布后，英伟达股价大涨逾 30%。Nvidia said on November 23 that its board had voted unanimously to reinstate Jensen Huang as president, chief executive officer and board director, effective immediately. Huang resigned on November 12 after admitting to extramarital affairs, one involving a woman alleged to have spied for China; the board said that after consulting senior U.S. government officials, it concluded his return posed no national security risk. An independent investigation into whether Huang was targeted by a foreign intelligence operation will continue, and the company will form a National Security and Export Control Committee and hire a chief compliance officer. Interim CEO Colette Kress returns to her role as CFO. Nvidia shares surged more than 30% on the news.

美方披露：半年内批准逾 87.9 万颗英伟达与 AMD 的 AI 芯片对华出口许可U.S. approved export licences for more than 879,000 Nvidia and AMD AI chips bound for China in six months

据美国众议院外交事务委员会 11 月 19 日披露的数据，2025 年 12 月至 2026 年 5 月间，美国商务部批准了逾 87.9 万颗英伟达 H200 与 AMD MI325X 等先进 AI 芯片对华出口许可，而同期申请总量超过 340 万颗。获批对象包括腾讯 15 万颗、阿里巴巴 7.5 万颗、百度 4.3 万颗、比亚迪 4000 颗，均为五角大楼认定的涉军企业。英伟达回应称，相关许可附带严格条件且数量有限，并表示已停止向中国发运 H200。The U.S. Commerce Department approved export licences for more than 879,000 advanced AI chips — Nvidia's H200 and AMD's MI325X — bound for China between December 2025 and May 2026, according to data released on November 19 by the House Foreign Affairs Committee. Companies had sought permission to ship more than 3.4 million chips in the period. Approved buyers included Tencent (150,000), Alibaba (75,000), Baidu (43,000) and BYD (4,000), all designated by the Pentagon as Chinese military companies. Nvidia said the licences carried strict conditions and limited volumes, and that it has since stopped shipping H200s to China.

台积电收紧涉华订单：16 纳米及以下制程须持美国出口许可TSMC curbs China-linked orders: U.S. export licence now required at 16nm and below

据报道，台积电已通知部分涉华客户，除非持有美国出口许可，否则将暂停 16 纳米及以下制程的订单。此举跟进美国商务部收紧的出口管制新规。分析认为，限制全球最大代工厂的先进制程产能，可能影响中国 AI 芯片设计公司的研发进度，并改变先进产能在客户之间的分配。TSMC has told some China-linked customers that orders at 16nm and below will be halted unless they hold a U.S. export licence, according to reports, following tighter U.S. Commerce Department rules. Limiting access to the world's largest foundry's leading-edge capacity could affect Chinese AI chip designers' development schedules and how advanced capacity is shared among other customers.

AI · 安全AI · Safety

OpenAI 首席科学家公开内部预训练损失曲线，呼吁全行业设共享 AI 发展上限OpenAI chief scientist publishes internal pretraining loss curve and calls for shared AI development limits

OpenAI 首席科学家 Jakub Pachocki 公开了公司内部的代码困惑度曲线：GPT-3.5 约为 0.99，GPT-4 约为 0.91，如今的前沿模型已低于 0.8。他提出，在监测手段完善之前，各前沿实验室应就困惑度等指标设定共享上限，并把新的网络安全与数学能力与具体损失水平挂钩讨论。据报道，该曲线还显示存在一个尚未发布的内部模型。OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki has published the company's internal code-perplexity curve: about 0.99 for GPT-3.5, about 0.91 for GPT-4, and below 0.8 for today's frontier models. He proposed that frontier labs agree on shared perplexity-based limits until monitoring improves, linking new cyber and mathematics capabilities to specific loss levels. The curve also appears to show an unreleased internal model beyond GPT-6.2 Astra, according to reports.

AI · 芯片AI · Chips

AI 智能体从零设计出新型 CPU，模拟能效比称达同类芯片 4.59 倍AI agents design a novel CPU from scratch, claiming 4.59 times the performance per watt of a comparable chip

据报道，初创公司 Architect Labs 让 AI 智能体从零设计出一款通用处理器 Frostbite，采用全新指令集与并行任务拆分方式。在 25 项工作负载的模拟测试中，该设计在 7 纳米预硅阶段的每瓦性能据称为同等规模传统顺序 CPU 的 4.59 倍，未经 AI 专项调优的 15 项负载上为 3.83 倍。该芯片尚未流片，也未公开对照 CPU 的具体型号，仍待独立验证。Startup Architect Labs says AI agents have designed a general-purpose processor, Frostbite, from scratch, with a new instruction set and a different way of splitting programs into parallel pieces. In simulated tests across 25 workloads, the pre-silicon 7-nanometre design reportedly delivered 4.59 times the performance per watt of a similarly sized conventional in-order CPU — 3.83 times on the 15 workloads that received no AI-specific tuning. The chip has not been fabricated, and the comparison CPU has not been publicly named, so the claims await independent examination.

AI · 研究AI · Research

MIT 小模型可在笔记本上设计模拟芯片，87.1% 任务八轮内达标MIT's small model designs analog chips on a laptop, solving 87.1% of tasks within eight turns

MIT 研究人员 11 月 23 日公布了 SLIC 训练方法，可让仅数十亿参数的小语言模型在笔记本上完成模拟电路设计，在八轮仿真内达成设计目标的比例为 87.1%，优于一个 7630 亿参数的云端模型 48.5 个百分点，并接近 GPT-6-Astra 的表现。由于模型可在本地运行，企业无需把保密的电路与代工数据发往外部云服务。该论文为预印本，将在 NeurIPS 2026 上发表。MIT researchers unveiled SLIC on November 23, a training method that lets a language model of just a few billion parameters design analog circuits on a laptop, solving 87.1% of design tasks within eight turns — beating a 763-billion-parameter cloud model by 48.5 percentage points and coming close to GPT-6-Astra. Because the model runs locally, companies can train it on confidential circuit and foundry data without sending anything to an external cloud service. The paper is a preprint, to be presented at NeurIPS 2026.
