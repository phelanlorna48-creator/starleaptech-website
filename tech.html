Anthropic 首日收盘 97.17 美元、涨 17%，开盘一度冲破 2 万亿美元估值Anthropic closes first day at $97.17, up 17%, after opening briefly above a $2 trillion valuation

Anthropic 11 月 19 日在纳斯达克以代码 ANT 首日挂牌，开盘报 111.50 美元、较 83 美元发行价高出约 34%，盘中估值一度突破 2 万亿美元；收盘回落至 97.17 美元，仍较发行价上涨 17.1%。本次 IPO 募资 813.4 亿美元，叠加 260 亿美元私募配售后总募资达 1073 亿美元，创下史上最大 IPO 纪录；据纳斯达克数据，首日成交量超过 8 亿股，同样刷新纪录。Anthropic began trading on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT on November 19, opening at $111.50 — about 34% above its $83 offer price — and briefly carrying a valuation above $2 trillion before closing at $97.17, still up 17.1% on the day. The $81.34 billion offering was the largest IPO on record, and together with a $26 billion private placement the company raised $107.3 billion in total. Nasdaq data put first-day volume above 800 million shares, also a record.

美国 AI 芯片新规 11 月 19 日生效，台积电与三星暂停部分对华供货U.S. AI chip rule takes effect November 19 as TSMC and Samsung pause some China-bound shipments

美国商务部关于 AI 芯片远程使用的临时最终规则 11 月 19 日正式刊登于《联邦公报》并生效，要求向印尼、马来西亚、新加坡、泰国和巴西出口先进芯片须持许可证，并对向中国企业提供大规模算力的境外数据中心设限。据《金融时报》报道，台积电与三星已暂停向部分中国客户交付芯片以待许可；据 The Information 报道，月之暗面已取消一笔 1.5 亿美元、在印尼租用英伟达 B300 芯片的协议。The U.S. Commerce Department's interim final rule on remote access to advanced AI chips was published in the Federal Register and took effect on November 19, requiring licences to export advanced chips to Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Brazil, and restricting chips for data centers supplying large-scale compute to Chinese firms. The Financial Times reported that TSMC and Samsung have paused some deliveries to Chinese customers while they seek licences, and The Information reported that Moonshot AI scrapped a $150 million deal to rent Nvidia B300 chips in Indonesia.

英伟达三季度营收 1158 亿美元创新高，股价仍跌约 3%Nvidia posts record $115.8 billion quarter, yet shares fall about 3%

英伟达 11 月 18 日公布的三季度财报显示，营收 1158 亿美元、同比增长 103%，净利润 653 亿美元，均超市场预期；公司还将下一季度营收指引定为 1300 亿美元，高于分析师预期的 1251 亿美元。但股价 11 月 19 日仍下跌约 3%，延续黄仁勋 11 月 12 日辞职以来的跌势。临时 CEO Colette Kress 在首次全员会上呼吁员工“向前看”；据《华尔街日报》报道，美国证交会已就公司信息披露展开民事调查。Nvidia reported fiscal third-quarter revenue of $115.8 billion on November 18, up 103% year on year, with net profit of $65.3 billion, both beating expectations; it guided next-quarter revenue to $130 billion, above the $125.1 billion analysts expected. Shares still fell about 3% on November 19, extending the slide since Jensen Huang resigned on November 12. Interim CEO Colette Kress told staff at her first all-hands meeting to "move on," and The Wall Street Journal reported that the SEC has opened a civil probe into the company's disclosures.

OpenAI 发布 GPT-6.2 Astra 与 Sol，旗舰模型 API 降价 20%OpenAI ships GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol, cutting flagship API prices 20%

OpenAI 11 月 17 日发布 GPT-6.2 Astra 与 GPT-6.2 Sol：Astra 面向高难度专业工作，API 定价为每百万输入 token 8 美元、输出 40 美元，较上一代便宜 20%；Sol 面向日常与智能体任务，定价维持 2 美元与 10 美元。据 OpenAI 称，Astra 在 Agents' Last Exam 上比上一代高 5.2 分。随模型同步发布的安全系统卡将两款模型在网络安全能力上评为 Critical 级。OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol on November 17. Astra, built for demanding professional work, is priced at $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens — 20% less than its predecessor — while Sol, for everyday and agent workloads, stays at $2 and $10. OpenAI says Astra scores 5.2 points above GPT-6 Astra on Agents' Last Exam. The accompanying system card rates both models at the Critical level for cybersecurity capabilities.

微软 Ignite 把 Copilot 定位为“工作新操作系统”，Agent 365 受管智能体超 5000 万Microsoft recasts Copilot as the "new OS for work" at Ignite as Agent 365 passes 50 million agents

微软 11 月 17 日在 Ignite 2026 开幕演讲中把 Copilot 定位为“工作新操作系统”，整合聊天、协作、编程与常驻自主智能体 Autopilot；Agent 365 平台受管智能体已超过 5000 万个。微软同时推出 7 款自研 MAI 模型，包括编程模型 Code 2 与 Code 2 Flash；据微软称，Code 2 达到 GPT-6.1 Sol 约 98% 的编程水平，成本约为其 40%。自研芯片 Maia 300 据称每瓦 token 产出比主流 GPU 系统高 40%。Microsoft used the opening keynote of Ignite 2026 on November 17 to recast Copilot as the "new operating system for work," combining chat, collaboration, coding and a persistent autonomous agent called Autopilot; its Agent 365 platform now governs more than 50 million agents. Microsoft also unveiled seven in-house MAI models, including the coding models Code 2 and Code 2 Flash; the company says Code 2 reaches about 98% of GPT-6.1 Sol's coding quality at roughly 40% of the cost. Its Maia 300 chip is claimed to deliver 40% more tokens per watt than leading GPU-based systems.

阿里高管称 20GW 数据中心目标或下调，国内芯片投入将在 2027–2028 年放量Alibaba may revise its 20 GW data center target down as domestic chip spending ramps up in 2027–2028

阿里巴巴一位高管 11 月 19 日表示，全球算力政策变化可能导致公司下调 2032 年前数据中心容量超过 20GW 的目标。阿里云此前预计，相关扩张将带来每年约 2000 亿至 2600 亿元人民币的资本开支。高管同时表示，公司对国产芯片的重大投入将在 2027 年与 2028 年到来，旗舰 AI 处理器 Zhenwu V900 的性能据称为上一代的三倍。An Alibaba executive said on November 19 that changes in global compute policy may lead the company to revise down its target of more than 20 GW of data center capacity by 2032. Analysts had modeled capital spending of roughly 200 to 260 billion yuan a year on the expansion. The executive added that major investment in domestic chips is expected in 2027 and 2028, and said its flagship Zhenwu V900 AI processor delivers three times the performance of its predecessor.
