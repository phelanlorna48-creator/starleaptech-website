英伟达暴跌 14% 至 125.53 美元，创 2025 年 1 月以来最大单日跌幅Nvidia plunges 14% to $125.53 in its biggest one-day drop since January 2025

英伟达 11 月 20 日大跌 14.01%，收于 125.53 美元，为 2025 年 1 月 27 日以来最大单日跌幅，也是 2025 年 5 月 12 日以来最低收盘价。据路透社报道，美国证交会正在审查英伟达的会计操作；加州总检察长 11 月 19 日也就芯片是否流入中国及信息披露问题向公司发出传票。抛售蔓延全市场：标普 500 指数跌 3.82% 至 6902 点、跌破 7000 点大关，纳斯达克综合指数年内转跌，AMD 与博通均跌逾 12%。Nvidia fell 14.01% on November 20 to close at $125.53 — its largest one-day drop since January 27, 2025, and its lowest close since May 12, 2025. Reuters reported that the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission is examining Nvidia's accounting practices, and California's attorney general served the company a subpoena on November 19 over whether chips reached China and whether investors were misled. The sell-off spread across the market: the S&P 500 fell 3.82% to 6,902, below the 7,000 mark, the Nasdaq Composite turned negative for the year, and AMD and Broadcom each fell more than 12%.

台积电收紧对华 AI 芯片代工：16 纳米及以下订单暂停，一般限于 28/40 纳米TSMC tightens China AI chip curbs, pausing orders at 16nm and below

据路透社报道，台积电已暂停部分中国客户的先进制程代工订单，要求 16 纳米及以下工艺订单须取得美国出口许可证，中国芯片设计公司一般只能使用较老的 28 纳米与 40 纳米工艺。台积电表示将遵守美国商务部的新规。美国新规 11 月 19 日生效，堵住了中国设计公司此前无需单独许可即可获得先进代工服务的合规路径。TSMC has suspended some advanced manufacturing orders for Chinese customers and now requires U.S. export licences for orders at 16 nanometres and below, Reuters reported, with affected Chinese chip designers generally limited to older 28- and 40-nanometre processes. TSMC said it will comply with the new U.S. Commerce Department rules. The rules, which took effect on November 19, close a compliance route that had let Chinese designers access advanced foundry services without individual licences.

美众议院外委会披露：美国已批准向中国出口 87.9 万枚英伟达与 AMD AI 芯片U.S. approved export of 879,868 Nvidia and AMD AI chips to China, House panel discloses

美国众议院外交事务委员会 11 月 19 日公布的商务部许可数据显示，2025 年 12 月至 2026 年 5 月间，美国批准向中国企业出口 87.9 万枚英伟达 H200 与 AMD MI325X 先进 AI 芯片，涉及 21 张许可证；同期申请量超过 344 万枚。据披露，腾讯获批 15 万枚、阿里巴巴 7.5 万枚、百度 4.3 万枚、比亚迪 4000 枚，且所有获批芯片均标注未发货。Licence data released by the U.S. House Foreign Affairs Committee on November 19 shows the Commerce Department approved exports of 879,868 Nvidia H200 and AMD MI325X advanced AI chips to Chinese buyers between December 2025 and May 2026, across 21 licences; applications in the period covered more than 3.44 million chips. Tencent was approved for 150,000 chips, Alibaba for 75,000, Baidu for 43,000 and BYD for 4,000, with every approval marked as not shipped.

数据中心 · 政策Data Centers · Policy

美参议院以 62 比 33 通过许可改革法案，要求数据中心自付电网成本U.S. Senate passes permitting bill 62–33, making data centers pay their own grid costs

美国参议院 11 月 19 日以 62 票对 33 票通过《两党美国可负担与就业法案》，法案将加快能源项目与输电线路的联邦审批，并要求数据中心承担全部电网基础设施与输电服务成本。据参议院能源委员会称，数据中心未来十年将为输电成本贡献约 550 亿美元。法案现已提交众议院审议。The U.S. Senate passed the Bipartisan American Affordability and Jobs Act on November 19 by 62 votes to 33, speeding federal approvals for energy projects and transmission lines and requiring data centers to bear the full cost of grid infrastructure and transmission service. The Senate Energy Committee said data centers would contribute about $55 billion toward transmission costs over the next decade. The bill now goes to the House of Representatives.

韩国年出口首破 1 万亿美元，AI 芯片占前十月出口 43%South Korea's annual exports top $1 trillion for the first time as AI chips drive the surge

韩国产业通商资源部宣布，韩国 2026 年累计出口额于 11 月 19 日下午 2 时 09 分突破 1 万亿美元，创历史首次，成为继美国、中国、德国之后第四个年出口破万亿美元的国家。1–10 月半导体出口近 3968 亿美元、约为去年同期的三倍，占出口总额约 43%；全年出口预计达 1.17 万亿美元。South Korea's cumulative exports for 2026 passed $1 trillion at 2:09 p.m. on November 19, the trade ministry announced — the first time the country has reached the milestone, making it the fourth country ever to do so after the United States, China and Germany. Semiconductor exports totalled nearly $396.8 billion in January–October, almost triple a year earlier and about 43% of all exports; full-year exports are projected at $1.17 trillion.

据郭明錤产业调查：谷歌拟砍 2027 年博通设计 TPU 8i 出货约两成，转投联发科Google plans to cut 2027 Broadcom-designed TPU 8i shipments by about 20% and shift volume to MediaTek, Kuo survey says

据天风国际分析师郭明錤的产业调查，谷歌计划将 2027 年博通设计的 TPU 8i 系列出货量削减约 100 万颗、约占该系列预期出货量的 20%，削减集中在高价的 8i Max 型号；同时计划增加联发科设计的 TPU 8t 系列出货量，细节尚未最终确定。受消息影响，博通 11 月 20 日收跌约 12%。Google plans to cut 2027 shipments of its Broadcom-designed TPU 8i series by about one million units — roughly 20% of expected volume, concentrated in the higher-priced 8i Max model — and increase shipments of the MediaTek-designed TPU 8t series, according to TF International analyst Ming-Chi Kuo's latest industry survey, with details not yet final. Broadcom shares fell about 12% on November 20 on the report.
