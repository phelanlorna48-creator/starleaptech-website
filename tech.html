英伟达三季度营收 1158 亿美元翻倍，首份“后黄仁勋时代”财报超预期Nvidia revenue doubles to $115.8 billion in first report since Huang’s exit

英伟达 11 月 17 日公布 2027 财年第三财季财报：营收 1158.4 亿美元，同比增长 103%，高于市场预期的约 1091 亿美元；调整后每股收益 2.68 美元，高于预期的 2.48 美元；数据中心业务收入 1076 亿美元，同比增长 110%。公司预计第四财季营收 1300 亿美元（上下浮动 3%），高于市场预期，但毛利率指引降至 71%–72%。这是创始人黄仁勋 11 月 12 日辞职后公司的首份财报，由临时 CEO、现任 CFO 科莱特·克雷斯主持；财报公布后公司股价盘后上涨约 3%。董事会还批准将股票回购授权增加 1500 亿美元。Nvidia reported fiscal third-quarter 2027 results on November 17: revenue of $115.84 billion, up 103% year on year and above the roughly $109.1 billion Wall Street expected; adjusted earnings per share of $2.68, versus $2.48 expected; and data center revenue of $107.6 billion, up 110%. The company guided fourth-quarter revenue to $130 billion, plus or minus 3%, above consensus, but guided gross margin down to 71%–72%. It was the company’s first report since founder Jensen Huang resigned on November 12, led by interim CEO and CFO Colette Kress; shares rose about 3% in extended trading. The board also approved a $150 billion increase to the share repurchase authorization.

OpenAI 发布 GPT-6.2 Astra 与 Sol，旗舰模型降价两成OpenAI launches GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol, cutting flagship prices 20%

OpenAI 11 月 17 日发布 GPT-6.2 Astra 与 GPT-6.2 Sol：Astra 定位高难度专业任务，API 价格为每百万输入 token 8 美元、输出 40 美元，比上一代便宜 20%；Sol 定位日常任务，价格维持 2 美元与 10 美元不变。两款模型即日起向 ChatGPT Plus、Pro、企业版与教育版用户在 ChatGPT Work 与 Codex 中开放，并上架 API。据 The Decoder 报道，独立评测机构 Artificial Analysis 的智能指数中 Astra 排名第二，仅次于 Anthropic 的 Claude Fable 5.2；OpenAI 同时把 Astra 列为首个达到其“AI 自我改进高风险”阈值的公开模型。OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol on November 17. Astra, built for demanding professional work, costs $8 per million input tokens and $40 for output in the API — 20% less than its predecessor — while Sol, the everyday model, holds its pricing at $2 and $10. Both are available now to ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Enterprise and Edu users in ChatGPT Work and Codex, and through the API. According to The Decoder, independent tester Artificial Analysis ranks Astra second on its Intelligence Index, behind Anthropic’s Claude Fable 5.2; OpenAI also flagged Astra as the first publicly released model to reach its “High” threshold for AI self-improvement risk.

Anthropic 今日定价 IPO，认购近七倍超额、或高于发行价区间Anthropic prices IPO today after orders run to nearly seven times supply

据彭博社 11 月 17 日报道，Anthropic 的 IPO 认购已接近发行量的七倍，公司计划 11 月 18 日定价、11 月 19 日在纳斯达克以代码 ANT 挂牌。发行价区间为每股 70 至 78 美元，拟发售 8.25 亿股 A 类股，募资最高约 643.5 亿美元；据《华尔街日报》报道，定价可能高于 80 美元。据 PYMNTS 报道，银行定于美东时间 11 月 17 日下午 4 点停止接单。另有 260 亿美元私募配售同步进行，谷歌与英伟达各承诺出资 100 亿美元。Anthropic’s IPO has drawn orders for close to seven times the shares on offer, Bloomberg reported on November 17. The company plans to price the deal on November 18 and start trading on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT on November 19. The price range is $70 to $78 per share for 825 million Class A shares, raising up to about $64.35 billion; the Wall Street Journal reported the price could land above $80. According to PYMNTS, banks were due to stop taking orders at 4 p.m. ET on November 17. A separate $26 billion private placement is running alongside the offering, with Google and Nvidia each committing $10 billion.

微软 Ignite 开幕：Copilot 常驻智能体 Autopilot 上线，定位“工作操作系统”Microsoft Ignite opens with Copilot Autopilot, pitching Copilot as a work operating system

微软 11 月 17 日在旧金山开幕的 Ignite 2026 大会上发布新一代 Copilot：常驻智能体 Autopilot 开始向 Frontier 早期用户推出，可在 Teams 与 Outlook 中跟进邮件、推进项目而无需每次等待指令；人机协作空间 Workspaces 同步开放；Copilot 内可直接创建编辑 Word、Excel 与 PowerPoint 文件的 Office 功能正式全面上线。微软还发布了六款新模型，包括面向编码的 MAI-Code-2，以及与 Salesforce 联合推出的 Copilotforce 集成。Microsoft opened Ignite 2026 in San Francisco on November 17 with a new generation of Copilot: Autopilot, a persistent personal agent that can follow up on emails and keep projects moving in Teams and Outlook without waiting for each prompt, began rolling out to Frontier early-access customers, and Workspaces, a shared space for people and agents, opened alongside it. Office in Copilot — creating and editing real Word, Excel and PowerPoint files inside the Copilot app — is now generally available. Microsoft also introduced six new models, including MAI-Code-2 for coding, and the Copilotforce integration with Salesforce.

政策Policy

中国谴责美国 AI 芯片新规，东南亚数据中心股应声下跌China denounces new U.S. AI chip rule as Southeast Asian data center stocks slide

针对美国商务部 11 月 16 日出台的 AI 芯片“远程访问”新规，中国 11 月 17 日予以谴责，指责华盛顿“滥用出口管制措施”。新规要求向印尼、马来西亚、新加坡、泰国和巴西出口先进芯片须持许可证，并对向中国企业提供超 1200 万 TPP 算力的数据中心出口设限。据路透社报道，东南亚数据中心与芯片供应链公司股价 11 月 17 日应声下跌；泰国数据中心协会称，未来 12 至 18 个月预计进入泰国的约 300 兆瓦 GPU 算力面临不确定性。China on November 17 denounced the U.S. Commerce Department’s new “remote access” rule for advanced AI chips, accusing Washington of “abusing export control measures.” The rule, issued November 16, requires licenses to export advanced chips to Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Brazil, and restricts shipments to data centers supplying more than 12 million TPP of compute to Chinese firms. According to Reuters, shares of Southeast Asian data center and chip supply-chain companies fell on November 17; Thailand’s data center association said about 300 MW of GPU capacity expected to enter the country over the next 12 to 18 months now faces uncertainty.

Salesforce 与微软联手推 Copilotforce，把 CRM 接进 CopilotSalesforce and Microsoft team up on Copilotforce, bringing CRM into Copilot

Salesforce 与微软 11 月 17 日在 Ignite 大会上宣布 Copilotforce 合作：Salesforce 客户数据与销售工作流将接入微软 Copilot，销售人员可在 Copilot 内查询客户背景、分析商机并直接执行 CRM 操作；Salesforce Data 360 与微软 Fabric 之间实现双向零拷贝数据互通。Salesforce in Copilot 将进入公开预览，Salesforce in Teams 处于试点阶段；埃森哲担任设计与实施合作伙伴，并率先在自家全球销售组织内部署。Salesforce and Microsoft announced Copilotforce on November 17 at Ignite, bringing Salesforce customer context and sales workflows into Microsoft Copilot: sellers will be able to ask about accounts, analyze opportunities and take CRM actions inside Copilot, while Salesforce Data 360 and Microsoft Fabric connect through bidirectional zero-copy data access. Salesforce in Microsoft Copilot will enter public preview (beta) and Salesforce in Teams is in pilot; Accenture is the design and implementation partner and is deploying the integrations in its own global sales organization.
