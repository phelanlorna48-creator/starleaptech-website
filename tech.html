彭博社：英伟达 Blackwell 芯片现身上海数据中心，关联“芯片走私”疑云人物Bloomberg: Nvidia Blackwell chips found at Shanghai data center linked to chip-diversion figure

据彭博社报道，提交给中国监管部门的贷款文件显示，一台英伟达 GB300 NVL72 机架已安装在上海“长三角项目”数据中心，该项目与新加坡云服务商 Megaspeed 创始人 Alice Huang 有关联；美方本周刚把 Megaspeed 列入实体清单。另有文件披露，该数据中心还装有 GB200 服务器。英伟达未予置评；受消息影响，英伟达股价 11 月 27 日盘中由涨转跌约 2%。Bloomberg reported that loan documents filed with Chinese regulators show a Nvidia GB300 NVL72 rack installed at the Yangtze River Delta Project data center in Shanghai, a site linked to Megaspeed founder Alice Huang; Washington added Megaspeed to the Entity List this week. Separate filings disclosed GB200 servers at the site. Nvidia declined to comment, and its shares erased early gains to fall about 2% in morning trade on November 27.

芯片 · 存储Chips · Memory

华为自研 HBM 曝光：64 通道、4096 位接口，带宽密度翻倍Huawei's homegrown HBM revealed: 64 channels and a 4,096-bit interface, doubling bandwidth density

据彭博社报道，华为研究人员设计了一款定制 HBM 芯片，拥有 64 个通道和 4096 位数据接口，位宽为行业标准 HBM4 的两倍，计划明年 2 月在旧金山 ISSCC 会议上公布细节。三星与 SK 海力士合计占据全球 HBM 收入的 80% 以上，华为此举意在打造国产替代；铠侠则与英伟达合作，用固态硬盘为 GPU 扩容以降低对 HBM 的依赖。Bloomberg reported that Huawei researchers have designed a custom HBM chip with 64 channels and a 4,096-bit data interface — twice the width of the industry-standard HBM4 — with details due at the ISSCC conference in San Francisco in February. Samsung and SK hynix together hold more than 80% of global HBM revenue, and Huawei's move aims at a homegrown alternative; Kioxia, meanwhile, is working with Nvidia to let GPUs reach data on SSDs and ease reliance on HBM.

Anthropic 已完成史上最大规模 IPO：共发行 11.27 亿股 Class A 股票、募资 935 亿美元，加上 260 亿美元同步私募，总募资达 1195 亿美元。公司 11 月 19 日首日收于 97.17 美元，较 83 美元发行价上涨 17%，但上市五个交易日后股价已回落至 82.79 美元，跌破发行价。Anthropic has closed the largest IPO on record: 1.127 billion Class A shares sold at $83 each, raising $93.5 billion, plus a $26 billion concurrent private placement, for $119.5 billion in total. 42 州总检察长联手阻击 Character.AI：反对拍卖 2100 万用户私密聊天记录42 state attorneys general move to block Character.AI auction of 21 million users' private chats

美国 42 个州的总检察长联手向破产法院提出异议，反对 Character.AI 在 12 月 1 日拍卖平台及 2100 多万美国用户聊天记录的计划，其中 210 多万个账号属于未成年人。州方称拍卖对买家没有任何数据保护要求，唯一已披露的竞买方是一家 11 月 13 日才在特拉华州成立的公司，并要求法院否决其 35 万美元“分手费”。Attorneys general from 42 U.S. states have asked a bankruptcy court to block Character.AI's plan to auction its platform and the private chat histories of more than 21 million U.S. accounts — over 2.1 million registered to minors — on December 1. The states say the sale imposes no data protections on the buyer, and the only disclosed bidder is a company formed in Delaware on November 13; they also ask the court to reject its $350,000 break-up fee.

安全Security

微软确认 Agent Framework 遭供应链投毒：仓库仍关闭，1.26.0 及更早版本安全Microsoft confirms Agent Framework supply-chain poisoning; repository stays offline, 1.26.0 and earlier unaffected

微软 11 月 25 日确认，11 月 17 日有人未经授权向 PyPI 上传了 Agent Framework 的 1.26.1 与 1.26.2 两个版本，其中植入了窃取凭据的代码，两版本已下架。微软称其 GitHub 仓库仍处于关闭状态，待确认安全后才会恢复；1.26.0 及更早版本、.NET 包与 Semantic Kernel 均未受影响，用户应检查是否安装了受影响版本并轮换相关凭据。Microsoft confirmed on November 25 that two versions of its Agent Framework Python package, 1.26.1 and 1.26.2, were uploaded to PyPI without authorization on November 17 carrying credential-stealing code; both have been removed. The project's GitHub repository remains unavailable until Microsoft can confirm it is safe to restore. Version 1.26.0 and earlier, .NET packages and Semantic Kernel are unaffected, and users should check whether they installed the tainted versions and rotate exposed credentials.
