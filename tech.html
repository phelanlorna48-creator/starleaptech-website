特朗普威胁对海外建数据中心征「重税」，微软、亚马逊、谷歌海外 AI 投资承压Trump threatens a "tremendous tax" on data centres built overseas, putting Microsoft, Amazon and Google's AI investments under pressure

美国总统特朗普 11 月 20 日在白宫表示，不会允许美国公司在海外建设数据中心，否则将征收「重税」，但未说明税率、适用范围或法律依据。据 CNBC 与《新闻周刊》报道，微软、亚马逊与谷歌已宣布数百亿美元的海外云与 AI 投资，包括谷歌 11 月 4 日宣布的 150 亿美元马来西亚计划。财政部长贝森特 11 月 22 日表示，他尚未与特朗普讨论过这一计划。President Donald Trump said at the White House on November 20 that he would not allow U.S. companies to build data centres overseas, warning they would face a "tremendous tax" — without giving a rate, scope or legal basis. CNBC and Newsweek reported that Microsoft, Amazon and Google have already announced tens of billions of dollars in overseas cloud and AI investment, including Google's $15 billion plan for Malaysia announced on November 4. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said on November 22 that he had not discussed the plan with Trump.

OpenAI 常规维护酿 3.5 小时大宕机，ChatGPT 与 API 请求失败率最高达 75%Routine maintenance takes ChatGPT and the OpenAI API down for 3.5 hours, with failure rates peaking at 75%

11 月 21 日 13:48 至 17:21（太平洋时间），ChatGPT 与 OpenAI API 的请求失败率最高达 75%，持续 211 分钟。据 OpenAI 在状态页发布的事故说明，诱因是例行数据库维护导致认证系统的两个 Postgres 只读副本同时崩溃，波及登录、对话、搜索、文件上传与语音等功能。OpenAI 已暂停同类维护，并表示全部系统当晚已恢复正常。Between 1:48 and 5:21 p.m. Pacific time on November 21, requests to ChatGPT and the OpenAI API failed at rates peaking at 75%, for 211 minutes. In a post-incident write-up on its status page, OpenAI said routine database maintenance crashed both Postgres read replicas behind its authentication system, hitting logins, conversations, search, file uploads and voice mode. OpenAI has paused similar maintenance and said all systems were back to normal that evening.

芯片 · 机器人Chips · Robotics

马斯克确认特斯拉 AI5 芯片将用于 Optimus 机器人与超算，车辆继续使用 AI4Musk says Tesla's AI5 chip will power Optimus robots and supercomputers, while vehicles stay on AI4

据报道，马斯克 11 月 22 日在 X 上确认，特斯拉下一代 AI5 芯片将用于 Optimus 人形机器人与 AI 训练超算，而车辆阵容继续使用 AI4，因为 AI4 的自动驾驶表现已优于人类司机。AI5 采用 2 纳米工艺，算力约为 AI4 的 10 倍、配备约 96GB 显存，由台积电亚利桑那工厂与三星得州工厂生产，目标 2027 年量产。Elon Musk confirmed on X on November 22 that Tesla's next-generation AI5 chip will power the Optimus humanoid robot and the company's AI training supercomputers, while its vehicles will stay on AI4 hardware because AI4 already delivers self-driving performance safer than a human driver, according to reports. Built on a 2 nm process, AI5 is expected to deliver roughly 10 times the computing power of AI4 with about 96GB of memory, manufactured by TSMC in Arizona and Samsung in Texas, with high-volume production targeted for 2027.
