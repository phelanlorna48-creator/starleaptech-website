美国商务部出台新规，堵住 AI 芯片“远程访问”漏洞U.S. moves to close AI chip “remote access” loophole with new Commerce rule

美国商务部 11 月 16 日发布临时最终规则，堵住中国企业通过海外数据中心远程租用美国先进 AI 芯片的“远程访问漏洞”。据报道，新规要求：向为中国等受限国家企业提供超过 1200 万 TPP 算力的数据中心出口先进芯片须持许可证；同时向印尼、马来西亚、新加坡、泰国和巴西出口先进芯片也须申领许可证，美国总部企业可豁免。规则在《联邦公报》刊登后生效，现有运营商有 90 天申领许可。消息公布当日，英伟达股价盘中一度跌 2.9%，费城半导体指数跌 5.5%。据 Gizmodo 报道，新规还正式废止了拜登时期的《AI 扩散规则》。The U.S. Department of Commerce issued an interim final rule on November 16 to close the “remote access loophole” that let Chinese firms rent advanced American AI chips through overseas data centers. Under the rule, exporting advanced chips to data centers providing more than 12 million TPP of compute to companies in China and other restricted countries requires a license; shipments to Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Brazil also need licenses, with an exemption for U.S.-headquartered firms. The rule takes effect when published in the Federal Register, giving existing operators 90 days to obtain licenses. Nvidia shares fell as much as 2.9% intraday on the news, while the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index dropped 5.5%. Gizmodo reported the rule also formally rescinds the Biden-era AI Diffusion Rule.

英伟达今晚收盘后公布第三财季财报，临时 CEO 克雷斯首秀Nvidia reports Q3 results after the close today in first test for interim CEO Kress

英伟达将于美东时间 11 月 17 日收盘后公布 2027 财年第三财季财报（季度截至 10 月 25 日），电话会定于美东时间下午 5 点。这是黄仁勋 11 月 12 日辞职后公司首次财报，由临时 CEO、现任 CFO 科莱特·克雷斯主持。公司此前指引季度营收 1080 亿美元（上下浮动 2%）、毛利率约 74%；据 Saxo 援引彭博数据，市场预期营收约 1091 亿美元、调整后每股收益约 2.47 美元。Nvidia will report fiscal third-quarter 2027 results after the U.S. market closes on November 17, with the earnings call at 5:00 p.m. ET. It is the company's first report since Jensen Huang stepped down on November 12; interim CEO and CFO Colette Kress will lead the call. Nvidia guided to quarterly revenue of $108 billion, plus or minus 2%, and gross margin of about 74%. According to Saxo, citing Bloomberg, consensus expects revenue of about $109.1 billion and adjusted EPS of about $2.47.

TensorWave 与 OpenAI 签六年协议，提供 457MW AMD 算力TensorWave signs six-year deal to supply OpenAI with 457MW of AMD compute

TensorWave 11 月 16 日宣布与 OpenAI 签署六年协议，在美国多个数据中心为其提供 457 兆瓦专用 AI 算力，采用 AMD Instinct MI455X GPU 与 Helios 机架系统，单租户专属使用。这是 OpenAI 与 AMD 去年 10 月达成的 6 吉瓦战略合作中首个公开的新云（neocloud）合作方。首批算力预计 2027 年上半年上线，2028 年初完成全部署；合同金额未披露。TensorWave announced on November 16 a six-year agreement to supply OpenAI with 457 megawatts of dedicated AI infrastructure across multiple U.S. data center sites, powered by AMD Instinct MI455X GPUs in Helios rack-scale systems and operated as single-tenant capacity. It is the first publicly named neocloud supporting the OpenAI–AMD strategic partnership announced in October 2025, which covers 6 gigawatts of AMD GPUs. The first capacity is expected online in the first half of 2027, with full deployment by early 2028; the contract value was not disclosed.

百度三季度：AI 云收入增 94%，搜索广告跌 22%Baidu Q3: AI cloud revenue jumps 94% as search ads fall 22%

百度 11 月 16 日公布 2026 年第三季度财报：总营收 308 亿元，同比微降 1%；AI 云基础设施收入 82 亿元，同比大增 94%，其中 GPU 云收入增 191%；AI 相关业务合计 132 亿元、增长 37%，占百度核心业务收入的 53%。与此同时，在线营销收入 119 亿元，同比下滑 22%，反映搜索广告持续承压。Baidu reported third-quarter 2026 results on November 16: total revenue was RMB 30.8 billion, down 1% year on year, while AI Cloud Infra revenue jumped 94% to RMB 8.2 billion, with GPU Cloud up 191%. Its broader AI-powered business reached RMB 13.2 billion, up 37% and now 53% of Baidu General Business revenue. Online marketing revenue fell 22% to RMB 11.9 billion as search advertising remained under pressure.

芯片Chips

PFN 与 SEMIFIVE 合作开发 MN-Core L2000 推理芯片，三星代工PFN and SEMIFIVE to co-develop MN-Core L2000 inference chips with Samsung Foundry

日本 Preferred Networks 与 SEMIFIVE 11 月 16 日宣布合作开发新一代 AI 推理芯片 MN-Core L2000 系列，逻辑芯片由三星代工生产。该系列采用逻辑芯片与 DRAM 垂直堆叠的 3D 结构，目标内存带宽达到上一代 L1000 系列的两倍以上，计划 2027 年第一季度流片。双方正在芝加哥 SC26 大会联合展示该系列。Japan's Preferred Networks and SEMIFIVE announced on November 16 they will co-develop the MN-Core L2000 Series, a next-generation AI inference chip, with Samsung Foundry manufacturing the logic die. The series uses a 3D structure stacking logic chips vertically with DRAM, targeting more than twice the memory bandwidth of the MN-Core L1000 Series, with tape-out scheduled for the first quarter of 2027. The two companies are jointly showcasing the series at SC26 in Chicago.

Anthropic IPO 认购约六倍超额，11 月 18 日定价、19 日挂牌Anthropic IPO about six times oversubscribed ahead of November 18 pricing

据彭博社 11 月 12 日报道，Anthropic 的 IPO 认购已达约六倍超额。公司计划 11 月 18 日定价、11 月 19 日在纳斯达克挂牌，代码 ANT；发行价区间为每股 70 至 78 美元，拟发售 8.25 亿股 A 类股，募资最高约 643.5 亿美元，完全摊薄估值约 1.4 万亿美元。据 OANDA 报道，公司三季度营收约 170 亿美元，环比增长 47%，但多年算力与基建承诺合计约 9009 亿美元。Anthropic's IPO order book is about six times oversubscribed, Bloomberg reported on November 12. The company plans to price on November 18 and begin trading on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT on November 19, offering 825 million Class A shares at $70 to $78 each to raise up to about $64.35 billion, implying a fully diluted valuation of about $1.4 trillion. OANDA reported third-quarter revenue of about $17 billion, up 47% quarter on quarter, against multi-year computing and infrastructure commitments of about $900.9 billion.

英伟达发布四节点 DGX Spark HPC 套件，桌面可跑 7000 亿参数模型Nvidia unveils four-node DGX Spark HPC kit that runs 700B-parameter models on a desk

英伟达 11 月 16 日在芝加哥 SC26 大会上发布 DGX Spark HPC 开发者套件：四台 DGX Spark 系统以无交换机环形结构互联，每台搭载 GB10 Grace Blackwell 超级芯片，四节点合计内存 512GB、NVMe 存储 20TB。英伟达称，借助 NVFP4 量化，套件可在本地运行最高 7000 亿参数的模型（如 DeepSeek V4.1）。套件已开售，HPC SDK 安装指南即将推出。Nvidia introduced the DGX Spark HPC Developer Kit on November 16 at SC26 in Chicago: four DGX Spark systems linked in a switchless ring, each built on the GB10 Grace Blackwell Superchip, with 512GB of combined memory and 20TB of NVMe storage. Nvidia says NVFP4 quantization lets the kit run models of up to 700 billion parameters locally, naming DeepSeek V4.1 as an example. The kit is on display at SC26 and available for sale; an HPC SDK installation playbook is coming soon.
