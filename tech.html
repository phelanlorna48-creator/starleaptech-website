英伟达叫停向东南亚四国发运 AI 服务器，配合美国新出口管制Nvidia halts AI server shipments to four Southeast Asian countries under new U.S. export controls

据《金融时报》报道，英伟达已要求戴尔、超微等制造合作伙伴暂停向马来西亚、新加坡、泰国和印度尼西亚发运搭载其 AI 芯片的服务器。美国上周生效的新规要求向上述国家出口先进芯片须取得许可证，华盛顿意在堵住中国企业获取先进算力的转口通道。英伟达股价 11 月 25 日下跌 3.9%，收于 149.30 美元；目前尚不清楚停运是临时措施还是长期安排。Nvidia has asked manufacturing partners including Dell and Supermicro to halt shipments of servers carrying its AI chips to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia, the Financial Times reported. New U.S. rules that took effect last week require export licences for advanced chips bound for those countries, as Washington moves to close routes Chinese firms are believed to use to obtain advanced computing power. Nvidia shares fell 3.9% on November 25 to close at $149.30; it is unclear whether the halt is temporary.

OpenAI 承认 GPT-6.2 计费错误，用量上限将提前最多 40% 触顶OpenAI admits GPT-6.2 billing error; usage limits will now be hit up to 40% sooner

OpenAI 11 月 26 日承认，GPT-6.2 Astra 与 Sol 上线时计费错误，多数用户的使用量被错误打折。修正后，同样的工作量将消耗更多额度：Astra 用户比上周提前约 40% 触及上限，Sol 提前约 25%。OpenAI 表示不会追收差额，并为 Go、Plus、Pro 与 Business 订阅用户各补发一次额度重置。OpenAI said on November 26 that a launch-day billing error had incorrectly discounted GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol usage for most users. With the fix, the same work consumes more of a plan's allowance: users will hit their limits about 40% faster on Astra and 25% faster on Sol than last week. OpenAI said it will not charge for past usage and will add one banked allowance reset for Go, Plus, Pro and Business subscribers.

FCC 批准 SpaceX 部署 100 万颗卫星，打造太空 AI 数据中心FCC clears SpaceX to deploy 1 million satellites for AI data centers in orbit

美国联邦通信委员会 11 月 25 日批准 SpaceX 部署最多 100 万颗卫星，组成名为 Starmind 的太空数据中心星座，在轨处理 AI 工作负载。这是美国首次批准太空数据中心星座。按许可要求，SpaceX 须在 2033 年 11 月前发射首颗卫星，2035 年前部署 10%，2040 年前完成全部署；马斯克此前表示首批演示可能在 2027 年进行。The U.S. Federal Communications Commission on November 25 authorized SpaceX to deploy up to 1 million satellites forming Starmind, a constellation of orbital data centers that would process AI workloads in space — the first such authorization in U.S. history. The licence requires a first satellite by November 2033, 10% of the constellation by 2035 and full deployment by 2040; Musk has said demonstrations could begin in 2027.

1Password 证实后台遭入侵：客户姓名与邮箱被盗，密码库未受影响1Password confirms backend breach: customer names and emails taken, vaults untouched

1Password 11 月 25 日披露，攻击者 11 月 19 日控制了一名员工的企业 GitHub 账号，进而向后台服务植入恶意代码，下载了部分客户的姓名、登录邮箱、账号标识及创建时间等管理信息。公司强调，加密密码库、已存密码与密钥均未被触及，无需改密码；主要风险是针对性的钓鱼邮件，用户须警惕冒充 1Password 的邀请或找回请求。1Password disclosed on November 25 that an attacker took control of an employee's corporate GitHub account on November 19 and used it to plant malicious code in backend services, downloading administrative records — names, sign-in email addresses, account identifiers and creation dates — for a portion of customers. The company stressed that encrypted vaults, stored passwords and keys were never reached and no password changes are needed; the main risk is targeted phishing, and users should be wary of unexpected invitation or recovery messages.

DeepL 发布 Aure v2 语音翻译模型，翻译延迟降低 50%DeepL launches Aure v2 voice translation model, cutting delay by 50%

DeepL 11 月 26 日在东京首届 Unmute 峰会上发布 Aure v2 语音 AI 翻译模型，采用端到端架构将语音直接译为另一语言的语音，省去转录、翻译、合成三步。据公司内部测试，新模型翻译延迟中位数比上一代降低 50%，准确度与表现力同步提升。首发支持英、德、法、西、日、韩六种语言，DeepL Voice 用量 2026 年第三季度同比增长逾 8 倍。DeepL launched Aure v2, its voice AI translation model, on November 26 at its first Unmute Summit in Tokyo. The end-to-end model translates speech directly into speech in another language, removing the separate transcription, translation and synthesis steps. In internal tests, median translation delay fell 50% versus its predecessor, with accuracy and expressiveness improved. It launches in six languages — English, German, French, Spanish, Japanese and Korean — as DeepL Voice usage grew more than 8x year over year in Q3 2026.

铠侠发布 GP2 AI 固态硬盘，随机读取超 1800 万 IOPSKioxia unveils GP2 AI SSD with random reads above 18 million IOPS

铠侠 11 月 26 日发布 GP2 系列 AI 系统固态硬盘，GPU 可直接访问，512 字节随机读取性能超过 1800 万 IOPS，约为上一代 GP1 的两倍。新品采用第三代 XL-FLASH 与 PCIe 6.0，下一代目标为 1 亿 IOPS；评估样品计划 2027 年底前向有限客户出货。同日铠侠还与英伟达联合演示，用 144 块 7.68TB 固态硬盘在 GB200 NVL72 上处理 2 万亿向量数据库。Kioxia announced the GP2 Series SSD for AI systems on November 26, with GPUs able to access it directly. Its 512-byte random read performance exceeds 18 million IOPS, roughly double the previous-generation GP1. The drive uses third-generation XL-FLASH and PCIe 6.0, with the next generation targeting 100 million IOPS; evaluation samples are planned for limited customers by the end of 2027. The same day, Kioxia and Nvidia jointly demonstrated 144 7.68 TB SSDs processing a 2-trillion-vector database on a GB200 NVL72.

出口管制冲击中国 AI 模型：智谱 GLM-5.5 延期，Kimi K4 或推迟至 2027 年Export controls hit Chinese AI models: Zhipu's GLM-5.5 slips, Kimi K4 may slip to 2027

Zhipu denied the delay, saying GLM-5.5 will ship in December.
