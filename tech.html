黄仁勋考虑重返英伟达 CEO，盟友组建“作战室”游说董事会Jensen Huang weighs a return as Nvidia CEO as allies lobby the board

据英国《金融时报》11 月 15 日报道，英伟达前 CEO 黄仁勋在 11 月 12 日辞职仅四天后，已与亲信讨论重返 CEO 一职；一位盟友正组建由硅谷高管组成的“作战室”，游说董事会并争取白宫支持。据路透社报道，马斯克与 Epic Games 首席执行官蒂姆·斯威尼已支持一封公开信，呼吁董事会“让黄仁勋回来”；美国财长贝森特同日则表示，英伟达需要“大幅整顿”，应从外部聘请 CEO。黄仁勋不持有控股权，无法推翻董事会决定；董事会尚未就继任者表态。英伟达将于 11 月 17 日公布第三财季财报。The Financial Times reported on November 15 that Jensen Huang, who stepped down as Nvidia's CEO on November 12, has discussed returning to the top job with confidants just four days after his exit. One ally is assembling a "war room" of Silicon Valley executives to lobby the board and seek White House support, the FT said. Reuters reported that Elon Musk and Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeney have backed a public letter urging the board to "Bring Jensen Back," while U.S. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said the same day that Nvidia needs "a dramatic shakeup" and should hire an outside chief. Huang holds no controlling stake and cannot overrule the board, which has not commented on a successor. Nvidia reports third-quarter results on November 17.

三星拟在得州奥斯汀为 SpaceX 生产星链卫星芯片，投资 19 亿美元、目标 2030 年投产Samsung to make Starlink satellite chips for SpaceX in Texas with a $1.9 billion investment

据《首尔经济日报》英文版报道，三星电子计划在美国得州奥斯汀工厂生产 SpaceX 星链卫星使用的 STM32V8 微控制器，该芯片由三星与意法半导体联合开发、采用 18 纳米 FD-SOI 工艺，用于星链卫星间的激光通信。三星将投资 19 亿美元扩建奥斯汀工厂，目标 2030 年投产；三星表示无法证实与个别客户的合同。Samsung Electronics plans to produce the STM32V8 microcontroller used in SpaceX's Starlink satellites at its Austin, Texas fab, the Seoul Economic Daily reported. Co-developed by Samsung and STMicroelectronics on an 18-nanometer FD-SOI process, the chip drives laser links between Starlink satellites. Samsung will invest $1.9 billion to expand the Austin plant, targeting production by 2030. Samsung said it could not confirm contracts with individual customers.

SK 海力士五年投入 1.4 万亿韩元扶持本土芯片供应商，强化 AI 供应链SK Hynix to spend $1 billion over five years backing local chip suppliers

据路透社报道，SK 海力士 11 月 16 日表示，未来五年将投入 1.4 万亿韩元（约 10.5 亿美元）支持韩国本土半导体材料、零部件与设备供应商，以强化 AI 芯片竞争中的供应链。公司还将与中央及地方政府合资建设一座 8746 亿韩元的测试设施 Trinity Fab，计划 2027 年 5 月启用。SK Hynix said on November 16 it will spend 1.4 trillion won (about $1.05 billion) over five years to support South Korean suppliers of semiconductor materials, parts and equipment, Reuters reported, aiming to strengthen its supply chain as AI chip competition intensifies. It will also co-fund an 874.6 billion won testing facility, Trinity Fab, scheduled to open in May 2027.

Anthropic IPO 拟 11 月 18 日定价、19 日纳斯达克挂牌，发行价区间 70 至 78 美元Anthropic IPO to price November 18 and debut on Nasdaq November 19 at $70–$78 a share

据 TechCrunch 报道，Anthropic 在 11 月 10 日更新的招股文件中将 IPO 发行价区间定为每股 70 至 78 美元，拟发售 8.25 亿股 A 类股，募资 577.5 亿至 643.5 亿美元；按区间上限计算，公司完全摊薄估值约 1.404 万亿美元。据公司文件与知情人士透露，公司计划 11 月 18 日定价、11 月 19 日在纳斯达克挂牌，代码 ANT；谷歌、英伟达等战略伙伴将另行认购约 260 亿美元无投票权股份。Anthropic set its IPO price range at $70 to $78 a share in an updated filing on November 10, TechCrunch reported, offering 825 million Class A shares to raise $57.75 billion to $64.35 billion — implying a fully diluted valuation of about $1.404 trillion at the top of the range. According to company filings and people familiar with the timetable, the shares are expected to price on November 18 and begin trading on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT on November 19. Google, Nvidia and other strategic partners will buy about $26 billion of non-voting shares in a separate placement.

政策Policy

美韩日等九国签署《旧金山宣言》，共建 AI 供应链联盟 Pax SilicaU.S. and eight allies sign San Francisco Declaration to build AI supply chain alliance Pax Silica

据 Aju Press 报道，美国与韩国、日本、澳大利亚、英国、以色列、印度、卡塔尔、阿联酋等八国 11 月 15 日签署《旧金山安全繁荣基础宣言》，共同保护半导体、关键矿产与能源等 AI 相关技术与供应链；该倡议被称为 Pax Silica。德国、瑞典、荷兰与欧盟委员会预计下周签署。美国总统特朗普定于 11 月 19 日在深圳 APEC 峰会期间会见中国国家主席习近平。The United States and eight allies — South Korea, Japan, Australia, the United Kingdom, Israel, India, Qatar and the United Arab Emirates — signed the San Francisco Declaration on a Secure Foundation for Sustainable Prosperity on November 15, Aju Press reported, pledging to jointly protect AI-related technologies and supply chains spanning semiconductors, critical minerals and energy. The initiative is known as Pax Silica. Germany, Sweden, the Netherlands and the European Commission are expected to sign next week. U.S. President Donald Trump is scheduled to meet Chinese President Xi Jinping in Shenzhen on November 19 during the APEC summit.

阿里巴巴拟分拆平头哥赴港独立上市，普冉股份筹备发行 H 股Alibaba to spin off chip unit T-Head for Hong Kong listing; Puya plans H shares

据 TrendForce 报道，阿里巴巴 11 月 10 日宣布分拆全资芯片子公司平头哥，申请在香港联交所主板独立上市；平头哥自研的真武 810E AI 芯片已于 2026 年 1 月发布，截至 4 月累计出货 56 万颗。同日，普冉股份宣布筹备发行 H 股赴港上市；芯德半导体同日在港交所挂牌。Alibaba announced on November 10 that it will spin off its wholly owned chip unit T-Head for an independent listing on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange, TrendForce reported. T-Head's self-developed Zhenwu 810E AI chip launched in January 2026, with cumulative shipments reaching 560,000 units by April. On the same day, Puya Semiconductor said it would prepare an H-share listing in Hong Kong, while XINDE SEMI debuted on the HKEX.

AI 数据中心抢芯片，印度车企采购成本承压、车用内存价格或大涨AI data centers squeeze chip supply as Indian automakers face rising costs

据 Data Street 报道，AI 数据中心需求激增正收紧全球芯片供应，印度车企面临半导体采购成本上升的压力：塔塔汽车、马恒达等厂商采购难度加大，两轮电动车厂商 Ather 称芯片价格持续上涨。据 S&P Global Mobility 预测，2026 年车用 DRAM 新合约价格可能较 2025 年上涨 70% 至 100%，供应紧张或持续至 2027 年。Surging demand from AI data centers is tightening global chip supply and pushing up semiconductor procurement costs for Indian automakers, Data Street reported. Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles and Mahindra & Mahindra are finding chips harder to source, while electric two-wheeler maker Ather flagged persistently rising chip prices. S&P Global Mobility expects prices under new automotive DRAM contracts to rise 70% to 100% in 2026 versus 2025, with supplies constrained through 2027.
