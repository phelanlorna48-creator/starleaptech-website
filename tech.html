美国总统特朗普 11 月 24 日在白宫接受《纽约邮报》采访时表示，他曾考虑让美国政府像去年入股英特尔那样持有英伟达股份，但尚未向公司提出这一想法，「现在做可能还不算晚」。他同时表示，若有必要，赦免刚复职的英伟达首席执行官黄仁勋「一直是个选项」，但他认为不太可能需要。黄仁勋 11 月 23 日复职后，将于 12 月初接受国会质询。President Donald Trump told the New York Post in a White House interview on November 24 that he had considered the U.S. government taking a stake in Nvidia, like last year's investment in Intel, but had not raised the idea with the company — "it's probably not too late." He also said pardoning reinstated Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang was "always an option" if needed, though he doubted it would be necessary. AI · 科研AI · Research

OpenAI 称 GPT-6 能设计蛋白质，实验室验证 322 个设计中有 35 个结合成功OpenAI says GPT-6 can design proteins, with 35 of 322 designs binding in lab tests

OpenAI 11 月 24 日发表预印本论文称，GPT-6 系列模型仅凭推理与代码即可设计新的蛋白质结构与氨基酸序列，无需调用专业生物模型。在与合作方 Onava 的实验室测试中，GPT-6.2 设计的 322 个迷你结合蛋白中有 35 个（10.9%）与目标蛋白结合成功，覆盖全部 5 个测试靶点。论文尚未经过同行评审，作者也承认这仍未证明无需专业模型辅助的端到端从头设计。OpenAI published a preprint on November 24 saying its GPT-6 family can design new protein structures and amino-acid sequences using only reasoning and code, without calling on specialized biology models. In lab tests with research partner Onava, 35 of 322 minibinders designed by GPT-6.2 — 10.9% — showed evidence of binding to their targets, across all five targets tested. The paper is not peer-reviewed, and its authors acknowledge it does not yet prove end-to-end de novo design without specialist models.

AI · 开源AI · Open Source

PrismML 发布三元权重模型 Bonsai 2.5，270 亿参数压缩至 5.9GBPrismML releases ternary-weight model Bonsai 2.5, squeezing 27 billion parameters into 5.9 GB

PrismML 11 月 24 日发布开源权重模型 Ternary Bonsai 2.5 27B，基于阿里巴巴 Qwen3.8 27B，采用三元权重（每个权重仅为 -1、0 或 +1），将 270 亿参数模型压缩至 5.9GB，可在个人设备上运行。据报道，该模型面向智能体编程与私有知识工作，采用 Apache 2.0 协议；其性能分数均为公司自测，尚无独立评测。PrismML released Ternary Bonsai 2.5 27B on November 24, an open-weight model built on Alibaba's Qwen3.8 27B that uses ternary weights — each weight is -1, 0 or +1 — to compress a 27-billion-parameter model into a 5.9 GB footprint that runs on personal devices. According to reports, the model targets agentic coding and private knowledge work under the Apache 2.0 licence; its benchmark scores are the company's own, with no independent evaluation yet.
