OpenAI 推出 ChatGPT Wallet：AI 智能体可代用户完成支付OpenAI launches ChatGPT Wallet, letting AI agents pay on your behalf

OpenAI 于 11 月 13 日推出 ChatGPT Wallet，让其个人智能体 dots 能代用户完成购物结账。据 9to5Mac 与路透社报道，该钱包与美国运通、万事达卡和 Visa 合作打造，初期仅面向年满 18 岁的美国 ChatGPT Pro 订阅用户；每笔购买均须用户确认商品、金额与卡片，并通过面容 ID 或指纹等生物识别验证。支付时卡号经令牌化处理，不会交给智能体或商家，卡片原有的积分与争议保障保持不变。OpenAI launched ChatGPT Wallet on November 13, letting its personal agents, dots, complete checkouts on a user's behalf. Built with American Express, Mastercard and Visa, the wallet is rolling out first to ChatGPT Pro subscribers aged 18 and over in the United States, 9to5Mac and Reuters reported. Every purchase must be reviewed and approved by the user, with a biometric check such as Face ID or a fingerprint. Card details are tokenized and never shared with the agent or merchant, and existing card rewards and dispute protections remain intact.

AMD 周五大跌 8.75%，但五日相对英伟达跑赢幅度创 1999 年来最大AMD falls 8.75% on Friday, yet its five-day lead over Nvidia is the widest since 1999

据 BeInCrypto 报道，AMD 股价 11 月 13 日收跌 8.75% 至 534.06 美元，英伟达跌 2.01% 至 162.85 美元。但过去五个交易日 AMD 累涨 44.7%、英伟达跌 8.5%，两者 53.2 个百分点的差距为自英伟达 1999 年上市以来最大。英伟达将于 11 月 17 日发布第三财季财报，市场将首次听到临时 CEO 科莱特·克雷斯的展望。AMD fell 8.75% to $534.06 on Friday, November 13, while Nvidia slipped 2.01% to $162.85, BeInCrypto reported. Over the previous five trading days, though, AMD gained 44.7% against Nvidia's 8.5% drop — a 53.2-point gap, the widest since Nvidia went public in 1999. Nvidia reports fiscal third-quarter earnings on November 17, when interim CEO Colette Kress will face investors for the first time.

Gemini 4 Carbon 登顶 MathArena 数学榜，单题成本约为对手 15 倍Gemini 4 Carbon tops the MathArena maths leaderboard at about 15x the cost per problem

据 AIDailyPost 报道，谷歌 Gemini 4 Carbon 以 85.4% 的预期表现登上 MathArena 数学基准榜首，领先 Anthropic 的 Claude Fable 5.2（82.8%）与 OpenAI 的 GPT-6.1 Sol（80.6%），成为一段时间以来首个登顶的非 OpenAI 模型。不过每解一道题 Carbon 的成本约为 15.70 美元，而 Sol 仅为 1.07 美元，榜单成绩尚未经过真实任务场景的检验。Google's Gemini 4 Carbon has topped the MathArena maths benchmark with 85.4% expected performance, AIDailyPost reported, ahead of Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.2 at 82.8% and OpenAI's GPT-6.1 Sol at 80.6% — the first non-OpenAI model to lead the table in a while. But each problem costs about $15.70 to run on Carbon versus $1.07 on Sol, and the leaderboard result has yet to be tested on real-world tasks.

黄仁勋离任余波：东南亚 AI 数据中心热潮面临更严审视Huang exit aftershock puts Southeast Asia's AI data center boom under scrutiny

据 Political.org 报道，黄仁勋离任后，新加坡与马来西亚的芯片与数据中心行业正面临更严格的审视：新加坡 11 月 13 日在一宗 AI 服务器欺诈案中对第五名嫌疑人提出 41 项指控；涉案云计算公司 Megaspeed 自 2025 年 2 月起接受新加坡当局调查。马来西亚贸易部数据显示，该国自 2021 年以来已批准 3,857 亿林吉特（约 946.7 亿美元）的数据中心相关投资。Southeast Asia's chip and data center industries face tougher scrutiny after Jensen Huang's exit, Political.org reported. Singapore charged a fifth person on November 13 with 41 counts in an AI server fraud case, and cloud firm Megaspeed has been under investigation by Singapore authorities since February 2025. Malaysia's trade ministry says the country has approved 385.7 billion ringgit (about $94.67 billion) of data center-related investment since 2021.

欧洲议会收到云与 AI 发展法草案报告，目标五至七年内数据中心容量增至三倍European Parliament receives draft report on cloud and AI act, aiming to triple data center capacity

据 TecniForge 报道，欧洲议会 11 月 11 日收到关于欧盟《云与 AI 发展法案》（CADA）的联合草案报告，推动统一的云与 AI 基础设施规则、扶持欧洲本土供应商、减少对外国超大规模云厂商的依赖。欧盟委员会的目标是在五至七年内将欧盟数据中心容量增至三倍；目前三家非欧洲超大规模厂商占据欧洲云市场 70% 以上。委员会定于 12 月 1 日讨论该报告。The European Parliament received a joint draft report on the EU's proposed Cloud and AI Development Act (CADA) on November 11, TecniForge reported, pushing for common cloud and AI infrastructure rules, more support for European providers and less reliance on foreign hyperscalers. The Commission aims to triple EU data center capacity within five to seven years; three non-European hyperscalers now hold more than 70% of the European cloud market. Committees will discuss the report on December 1.

印度支付巨头 Razorpay 获 SEBI 上市批准，拟募资 500 亿至 600 亿卢比Indian payments major Razorpay wins SEBI nod for IPO of up to ₹6,000 crore

据《北印度政治家报》报道，印度支付公司 Razorpay 已获印度证监会 SEBI 的最终观察函，扫清上市监管障碍，拟募资 500 亿至 600 亿卢比（₹5,000–6,000 crore），目标估值约 50 亿美元。公司 6 月选择了 SEBI 的保密预递交路径，预计未来数周提交更新版招股书草案，2026 年 12 月至 2027 年第一季度在孟买挂牌。Indian payments company Razorpay has received final observations from market regulator SEBI, clearing the way for its IPO, the North India Statesman reported. The issue is expected to be sized at ₹5,000–6,000 crore, targeting a valuation of about $5 billion. Razorpay opted for SEBI's confidential pre-filing route in June and is expected to file its updated draft prospectus in the coming weeks, aiming for a listing between December 2026 and the first quarter of 2027.
