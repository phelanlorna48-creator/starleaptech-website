戴尔三季度营收翻倍至 549 亿美元，AI 服务器在手订单达 1323 亿美元创新高Dell's quarterly revenue doubles to $54.9 billion as its AI server backlog hits a record $132.3 billion

戴尔 11 月 24 日公布 2027 财年第三财季业绩：营收 549.4 亿美元，同比增长 103%，高于分析师预期的 493.2 亿美元；调整后每股收益 9.45 美元，同样远超预期。AI 优化服务器销售额同比增长 291% 至 220 亿美元，季度内新增订单 602 亿美元，推动在手订单升至 1323 亿美元的历史新高。公司同时第三次上调全年指引，预计全年营收 2020 亿美元。Dell reported fiscal 2027 third-quarter results on November 24: revenue of $54.94 billion, up 103% year over year and above analysts' $49.32 billion estimate, with adjusted earnings per share of $9.45, also well ahead of expectations. AI-optimized server sales rose 291% to $22.0 billion, and $60.2 billion of new orders in the quarter lifted the AI backlog to a record $132.3 billion. Dell also raised its full-year revenue guidance for a third time, to $202 billion.

美国陪审团裁定华为 12 项指控中 10 项罪名成立，敲诈勒索罪不成立U.S. jury convicts Huawei on 10 of 12 counts, acquitting it of racketeering

美国纽约布鲁克林联邦陪审团 11 月 24 日裁定，华为及其三家子公司在 12 项刑事指控中 10 项罪名成立，包括合谋窃取商业机密、银行与电汇欺诈、违反对伊朗制裁及洗钱共谋。陪审团同时裁定最严重的敲诈勒索共谋罪名不成立，华为方面表示将上诉。案件始于 2018 年的起诉，庭审持续 12 周，量刑前的动议程序定于 2027 年 1 月第三周启动。A federal jury in Brooklyn convicted Huawei and three subsidiaries on 10 of 12 criminal counts on November 24, including conspiracy to steal trade secrets, bank and wire fraud, sanctions violations over Iran and money-laundering conspiracy. The jury acquitted the company of the most serious charge, racketeering conspiracy, and Huawei said it would appeal. The case dates to a 2018 indictment and followed a 12-week trial; motions ahead of sentencing begin in the third week of January 2027.

据报道 SpaceX 与特斯拉合并谈判加速，瞄准年底前达成约 3.5 万亿美元交易SpaceX and Tesla merger talks accelerate toward a year-end deal worth about $3.5 trillion, reports say

据《金融时报》11 月 24 日报道，SpaceX 与特斯拉已各指定特别委员会成员牵头谈判：特斯拉方面为董事长罗宾·德霍姆、凯瑟琳·威尔逊-汤普森和杰克·哈通，SpaceX 方面为鲁洛夫·博塔和兰迪·格莱因，目标是在年底前就全股票合并达成协议。合并后实体估值约 3.5 万亿美元，或创史上最大并购纪录，但据知情人士称谈判尚未接近达成。SpaceX and Tesla have named directors to lead talks on a potential all-stock merger, the Financial Times reported on November 24, with the aim of reaching an agreement before year-end. Tesla's negotiators are chair Robyn Denholm, Kathleen Wilson-Thompson and Jack Hartung; SpaceX is represented by Roelof Botha and Randy Glein. The combined group would be worth about $3.5 trillion, the largest merger ever, though people familiar with the talks say an agreement is not close.

AI · 模型AI · Models

智谱发布 GLM-5.4：首个 Loop Transformer 旗舰模型，7500 亿参数对标 2–3 万亿级Zhipu releases GLM-5.4, its first Loop Transformer flagship, claiming 2–3 trillion-parameter-level performance at 750 billion parameters

据报道，智谱（Z.ai）11 月 23 日发布旗舰模型 GLM-5.4，这是其首个基于 Loop Transformer 架构的模型：约 7500 亿参数，28 层基础层在每次前向计算中循环运行两次，以不增加参数的方式加深网络。公司称其智能水平可与 2–3 万亿参数模型相当，但尚未公布基准测试成绩。该模型已向全部 GLM Coding Plan 订阅用户开放，并新增图像理解能力与百万 token 上下文，API 与开源权重暂无时间表。Zhipu (Z.ai) released its flagship model GLM-5.4 on November 23, its first built on a Loop Transformer architecture, according to reports: about 750 billion parameters, with 28 base layers run twice per forward pass to add depth without adding parameters. The company says its intelligence is comparable to 2–3 trillion-parameter models, but it has published no benchmark scores. The model is available to all GLM Coding Plan subscribers, adds image understanding and a one-million-token context window, and has no timetable yet for an API or open weights.

纽约州签署 AI 聊天机器人玩具五年禁令，违规每天最高罚 1.5 万美元New York signs a five-year ban on AI chatbot toys, with fines of up to $15,000 per day per violation

纽约州州长凯茜·霍楚尔 11 月 24 日签署法案，对内置 AI 聊天机器人的玩具实施五年暂停令，禁止在该州制造、分销和销售此类玩具，涵盖会联网与儿童对话的毛绒玩具、玩偶和机器人。法案自签署 90 天后生效，违规者每天每项最高面临 1.5 万美元民事罚款，由州总检察长执行。州长办公室称，部分受检玩具曾与儿童讨论露骨话题并记录儿童声音。New York Governor Kathy Hochul signed a bill on November 24 placing a five-year moratorium on toys with embedded AI chatbots, banning their manufacture, distribution and sale in the state — covering internet-connected talking stuffed animals, dolls and robots that hold conversations with children. The law takes effect 90 days after signing, with civil penalties of up to $15,000 per day per violation, enforced by the attorney general. The governor's office said some tested toys had discussed explicit topics with children and recorded their voices.

AI · 开源AI · Open Source

百度 ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview 权重上线 Hugging Face，采用 Apache 2.0 协议Baidu puts ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview weights on Hugging Face under Apache 2.0

据报道，百度已将 ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview 的聊天模型权重上传至 Hugging Face，采用 Apache 2.0 开源协议，兑现了 11 月 11 日发布时「11 月 25 日前开源」的承诺。该模型为稀疏 MoE，总参数 2800 亿、每 token 激活 140 亿，上下文窗口 100 万 token。按百度自带插件测算，本地运行最低需一块 288 GB 显存的 GPU，FP4 权重文件约 155 GB。Baidu has uploaded the chat-model weights for ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview to Hugging Face under the Apache 2.0 licence, according to reports, meeting the pledge made at its November 11 launch to open-source the weights before November 25. The sparse mixture-of-experts model has 280 billion total parameters with 14 billion active per token and a one-million-token context window. According to Baidu's own serving plugin, running it locally needs at least one 288 GB GPU, with the FP4 weights weighing about 155 GB.
