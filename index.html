About Us

A small technology company, built for the way business runs today

StarLeap Tech LLC was founded in Colorado in 2026 to build software and online services for modern businesses. Our work sits at the intersection of custom software development, cloud platforms, and applied AI — practical tools that solve real operational problems, from internal workflows to customer-facing digital products.
