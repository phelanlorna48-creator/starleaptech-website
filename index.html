How We Work

A straightforward process, from idea to ongoing support

1 · Scope

You tell us what you need. We reply with a written scope, a fixed price or a clear rate, and a timeline — before any work starts.

2 · Build

We build in short iterations and show progress as we go, so there are no surprises at the end.

3 · Launch

We deploy, verify everything live, and hand over documentation and access. Nothing is "done" until you can open it yourself.

4 · Support

We stay available for fixes, updates and the next iteration — on terms agreed up front.
